Churn signal explorer

Pick a variable to see the churn rate in each group of subscribers. The dashed line is the overall rate. Bar width does not show group size; hover a bar for the count.

Takeaway. Billing state (auto-renew, cancellation, expiry date, plan length) splits subscribers into groups with churn rates near 0 % or near 100 %. Listening behaviour shifts the rate more gently: a silent streak of 15+ days multiplies it about five times, but most listening groups stay close to the average. The models then test whether listening still adds information once billing is known (see Results).
