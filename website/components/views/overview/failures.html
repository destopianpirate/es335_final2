Where the champion fails, and why

At τ = 0.45 the champion misses 1,484 of 17,029 test churners and raises 2,063 false alarms. These come mostly from four groups.

1. Plans expiring soon after the cutoff

recall 0.595

158,450 test subscribers have a plan expiring within 30 days, and only 1.85% of them churn. The renewal has not happened yet, so billing cannot show it. 1,187 of the 1,484 missed churners are here.

2. Auto-renew still on

36.6% of misses

Over a third of missed churners still had auto-renew switched on. Their billing looks exactly like a loyal subscriber's, and the model leans on billing.

3. Subscribers with no transactions

1,233 false alarms

7,248 subscribers have no billing history and 78% of them churn, so the model flags almost all of them. The 22% who stay make up most of the false alarms.

4. Active listeners who still leave

recall 0.892

Recall is 0.892 for subscribers with March listening, against 0.985 for those without. Someone who keeps playing music but does not renew gives the model little to go on.

Per-segment numbers and examples are in Errors. A time-based split, cutting listening at each expiry, and a model for soon-expiring plans are listed under What next.
