Could the model be seeing the answer?

Leakage is when a feature holds information the model would not have at prediction time. It makes scores look better than they will be in use. We checked each way it could enter.

What each cutoff can see

Plans expire during March, so a model frozen on 31 March already sees most renewals. Cutting billing at 1 March removes them. Click a model box for the evidence.
