11Limitations/ 16

What these results do not show

The 31 March scores are optimistic for early warning

The labelled plans expire during March, so the last March payment often already shows the renewal. The headline score means "easy once billing shows the decision", not "we can read intentions". With billing cut at 1 March the model is much weaker (see the strict check).

One snapshot, random split

Train and test come from the same month, so we do not show the model works in a later month. A split by time was not possible: only plans expiring in March 2017 are labelled, and the logs cover that month only. The scores hold for this month’s subscribers.

Hard cases stay hard

Prediction is not causation

Feature importance shows what the model uses, not what makes people leave. Turning auto-renew on would not necessarily keep someone; it is partly a sign of an earlier decision to stay. Testing that needs an experiment.

Listening data is thin

Only March 2017 is used, and about a fifth of subscribers have no logs. A longer history might show a larger effect.

Threshold and tuning use the same validation split

This makes validation scores slightly optimistic. Test scores are not affected, because test was never used for a choice.

Predictor page is approximate

The Live Predictor runs the real model, but asks for 16 inputs and fills in the rest. Treat it as an illustration, not a scoring tool.

Privacy and fairness

IDs are anonymised, with no names or contacts. But age and gender are inputs, and we did not check error rates by group. Before real use, audit those groups, and use scores to offer help, not to treat people worse.

The Kaggle test set has no labels

The competition test file lists subscribers without the answer. We predict them (results/submission.csv) but cannot score them, so every number here comes from our own test split.

12Conclusion/ 16

Answer to the question

Which number to quote

0.9644 is an upper bound: at 31 March, billing often already shows whether the subscriber renewed. 0.5682 [0.5610, 0.5757] is closer to an early warning: billing before 1 March plus March listening, 6.3× the no-skill level. It is not a pure 1 March prediction, because listening still runs to 31 March.

13What next/ 16

What we would do next

Split by time, not at random

Train on earlier months and test on a later one.

Cut listening at each subscriber's expiry

So the strict check has no listening after expiry.

Choose the threshold by cost

Set τ from the real cost of a lost subscriber against a wasted offer, not from F1.

A model for soon-expiring plans

Recall drops to 0.595 for plans expiring within 30 days. A model trained only on them could lean more on listening.
