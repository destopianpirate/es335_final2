What the champion relies on

Share of total split gain, top 12 features. billing, listening, demographic.

Split gain is global: it says which features the model uses most, not in which direction. For a single subscriber, the Live Predictor breaks the prediction into per-feature contributions: walking each of the 861 trees, the change in the node value at every split is credited to the feature used there, so the contributions plus a base value add up exactly to the predicted log-odds.

Why the 31 March score is optimistic

A subscriber churns if they do not renew within 30 days of their plan's expiry, and every labelled plan expires in March 2017. 95.4% of subscribers have a March transaction, and they churn at only 5.5%; the rest churn at 80.9%.

So by 31 March, a renewal is often already visible in billing. This is not leakage from the future (it is dated before the cutoff), but it means the model partly reads the decision instead of predicting it.

What the strict check changes, and what it does not

Notebook 08 rebuilds every billing feature from transactions dated before 1 March 2017, before any labelled expiry, and retrains the same tuned LightGBM on the same split. The threshold is re-chosen on validation.

Listening still covers all of March, so some of it may fall after a subscriber's expiry. Part of listening's +0.1474 may therefore still reflect the outcome.
